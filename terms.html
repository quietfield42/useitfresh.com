2. Food safety — please read

The app records dates you enter and, where there is none, estimates one from a typical shelf life. Estimates are labelled as estimates. The app does not inspect your food and cannot know how it has been stored or handled.

  • It never tells you food is safe, spoiled or fit to eat.
  • Meal ideas are written by a machine from the names of your food; they are suggestions, not recipes tested by a chef or checked by a dietitian.
  • Allergy and diet exclusions do their best to leave foods out, but they are not a medical check. Read the label on everything you cook with, every time.
  • Calorie and protein figures are estimates for guidance, not nutrition or medical advice.

Use your own judgement, follow the storage advice on the packaging, and when in doubt with high-risk food, throw it out.
