How it works

Three things, and none of them is typing out your whole fridge.

document_scanner
Put your food in

Photograph a shelf and it lists what it sees. Scan a barcode, photograph a receipt, or add something by hand. You check the suggestions before anything is added.

schedule

Every food shows how long it has, and says whether that came from a printed date or an estimate. A daily reminder names what to use next.

Questions

Does it tell me whether food is still safe to eat?

No, and it never will. It tracks dates and gives estimates so you notice food in time. Judging whether something is safe is yours to make — check the food itself, and when in doubt with high-risk food, throw it out.

What happens to the photos I take?

A photo of a shelf, a receipt or a single food is sent to Google's Gemini service to read what's in it, and the picture is stored in your household's private storage so your food has a picture. Receipt photos aren't kept. See the privacy policy.

Can my partner or housemates see the same kitchen?

Yes. Create a household and share its invite code. Everyone sees the same food, shopping list and plans, and changes appear on each other's phones straight away.

How does it learn what I like?

From choices you already make in the app: the meal ideas you keep, the meals you plan, cook and mark as eaten, and the ones you skip or swap out. Recent choices count most, so a phase doesn't stick forever. A short summary goes with your food list when you ask for ideas. You can read it, remove parts of it or reset it in Profile → What we've learned. It's part of Plus.
