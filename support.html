Support

Something not working, or an idea for the app? Write to us and a person will answer.

Email support@useitfresh.com

We aim to reply within two working days. It helps if you say which phone you have and what you were doing at the time.

Getting started

How do I put my food in?

Tap Scan. Choose Shelf to photograph a whole shelf, Barcode for a packaged food, Receipt to add a whole shop, or Photo for one food. You see everything it found and can fix it before anything is added. Enter manually adds a food by hand.

Why does a food say “estimate”?

Because nothing was printed on it. The app guesses from a typical shelf life for that kind of food and says so, rather than inventing a use-by date. Add the printed date on the food's screen and the guess is replaced.

Can I use it with my partner or housemates?

Yes. In Profile → Household, share your invite code. When someone joins, they see the same kitchen, shopping list and plans. Joining another household switches the app to that household; you can switch back in the same place.

Nothing is showing / it says it can't reach my kitchen

That is usually the connection. The app keeps what it last loaded and tries again. If it keeps happening on a good connection, email us and say roughly when it happened.

Photos and pictures

Where do the food photos come from?

Most come from our own library of photographs — a fresh, appetising picture of that kind of food. Some come from the photo you took, and some from the product's barcode (credited to Open Food Facts). In Profile → Food pictures you choose the order you prefer.

A food has the wrong picture, or none

Tell us the exact name of the food and we will fix the library — it updates without a new version of the app. On a food's screen you can also add or change its photo yourself.

Is a receipt photo kept?

No. It is read to get the lines and prices, then discarded. Card numbers are never read or stored.

Reminders

When does it remind me?

Once a day, late morning, naming the foods that need using. Change how many days' notice you get, or turn reminders off, in Profile → Reminders. If you plan meals, you also get a reminder on the day of a planned lunch or dinner.

I'm not getting reminders

Check notifications are allowed for Use It Fresh in your phone's settings, and that reminders are on in Profile → Reminders. Open the app once after turning them on so it can set them.

Account and data

How do I delete my account?

Email support@useitfresh.com from the address you signed up with, and we will delete your account, your food, your photos and your settings within 30 days.

Can I get a copy of my information?

Yes — ask us at the same address and we will send it to you.

What do you do with my information?

Only what is needed to run the app for you and your household. No ads, no analytics, nothing sold. The privacy policy sets out every detail.

Free and Plus

What costs money?

Nothing today — Plus is not on sale yet. When it is, the everyday things stay free: your kitchen, dates and reminders, allergy exclusions, meal ideas, barcodes and single-food photos.

Does Plus cover my household?

Yes. One Plus unlocks it for everyone in that household.

A word on food safety

Use It Fresh helps you keep track of food and dates. It cannot tell you whether food is safe to eat, and it never claims to. Use your own judgement, follow the storage advice on the packaging, and when in doubt with high-risk food, throw it out.
