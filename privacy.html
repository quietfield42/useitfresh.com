Privacy policy

Last updated 17 September 2026 · applies to the Use It Fresh app and this website

In short. Use It Fresh keeps the food you add, the photos you take of it, and the settings you choose. It is used to run the app for you and your household — nothing else. There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and we do not sell your information. You can ask for your account and everything in it to be deleted at any time.

What we collect

We do not collect your location, contacts, calendar or health data. The app asks for the camera, your photo library and notifications only when you use a feature that needs them, and you can refuse.

Who it is shared with

Only the services that make the app work:

  • Supabase — our database, sign-in, file storage and server functions. Everything above is stored there. Region: [TO CONFIRM].
  • Google (Gemini) — photos of food, shelves and receipts are sent to be read, along with the food names in your kitchen when you ask for meal ideas. On Plus, the meals you tend to choose or turn down are sent too, to write your taste summary and lean your ideas towards it; no name or email goes with them. Google processes them to answer the request.
  • Open Food Facts — when you scan a barcode or look for a product picture, that barcode or product name and your chosen country are sent to them. Nothing about you is sent.
  • OneSignal — delivers notifications to the other people in your household, for example when someone plans a meal or adds to the shopping list. It receives an ID for your account (not your name or email) and the words of the notification, which can include a first name and a food.
  • SendGrid — sends the emails your account needs, such as confirming your address or resetting your password. It receives your email address and that email.
  • Apple and Google — if you choose to sign in with them.

Your household's food, photos, lists and plans are visible to the other members of that household. That is the point of a shared kitchen, so only join a household you trust.

We do not sell your information, we do not share it for advertising, and there are no advertising or analytics tools in the app.

Photos you take

Photos of your food are kept in your household's private storage so your food has a picture. Photos of receipts are read and then discarded. Cut-out pictures of packaged products may also be kept with the product's name, barcode, country and date so the app can show a picture of that product to your household.

A future setting will let you choose to share pictures of packaged products beyond your household. It is off unless you turn it on, and it is not switched on in the app today.

Where it is kept, and for how long

Your information is kept while your account exists. Delete something in the app and it is removed from your kitchen; a small history of what happened to a food is kept so the app can show what you used and let you undo. Ask us to delete your account and we remove your profile, your food, your photos and your settings, and the household you own if no one else is in it, within 30 days. Backups roll off after that.

Your choices

  • See or correct your details in the app, under Profile.
  • Delete your account — email support@useitfresh.com from the address you signed up with and we will delete it.
  • Get a copy of your information — ask us and we will send it.
  • Turn off reminders in the app under Profile → Reminders, or in your phone's settings.
  • Complain — if you think we have mishandled your information, tell us first. In Australia you can then complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au); in the UK or EU, to your local data protection authority.

Children

Use It Fresh is for adults running a kitchen. It is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. A household can list children by first name for portion sizes; that is up to the adult who enters it, and no account is created for them.

Security

Sign-in is handled by Supabase, and everything travels over an encrypted connection. Database rules mean one household cannot read another household's kitchen. No service is perfectly secure, so if something goes wrong we will tell the people affected and the regulator where the law requires it.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date at the top, and say so in the app when the change matters.
